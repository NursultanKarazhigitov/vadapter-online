Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Vadapter AI (“we”, “us”) provides an AI agent that turns chat messages, voice notes, photos and PDF files into rows in a Google Sheet chosen by the user. This policy explains what data we process, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@vadapter.online.

Who we are

The data controller is We are against poverty LLP (BIN 260540006316), the company that operates Vadapter AI, registered in the Republic of Kazakhstan, Building 17, Sagynbay Nurgaliyev Street, Zheruyik Microdistrict, Atyrau 060000, Atyrau Region, Kazakhstan. Contact: support@vadapter.online.

Who this applies to

  • Account owners — people who sign in at app.vadapter.online and set up agents (for themselves or for clients).
  • End users — people who send messages to an agent in Telegram or in the web chat, including people who use an agent made by someone else.

Agents made by other people

An account owner (an “agent author”) can share an agent through a link. When you start such an agent, you sign in to Vadapter AI with your own account, connect your own Google account and use your own spreadsheet — a copy of the author’s template in your Google Drive, or a spreadsheet you already have.

  • What the agent author sees: your name and email, whether your spreadsheet is connected, the date of your last activity, and aggregate counts (for example, how many records were saved).
  • What the agent author never sees: your spreadsheet, the rows written to it, your messages, voice notes, photos or files, or your Google access.
  • The agent author can block your access to their agent. You can disconnect an agent or delete your data at any time on the “Connected agents” page or by sending /stop to the bot.

Data we process

Photos, PDF files and voice recordings are processed in memory and are not stored. The chat history keeps only a label such as “[photo]”. Draft records are stored encrypted and expire automatically.

How we use Google user data

When you connect Google, we request access only to the spreadsheets you pick yourself in Google’s file picker (drive.file). We use it only to:

  • read the tabs, header row and dropdown lists of the spreadsheet you selected;
  • when you choose to start from an agent’s template, make a copy of that template in your Google Drive and clear the author’s sample records in the copy;
  • write the rows that you (or your end user) confirmed on the review card.

We do not read, list or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

Vadapter AI’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models; it is not sold; it is not used for advertising; and it is not read by people except with your consent, for security purposes, to comply with the law, or when aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the service, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • OpenAI — understanding messages, transcribing voice notes and reading photos and PDFs. Data sent through the OpenAI API is not used to train their models.
  • Railway — application hosting and database.
  • Clerk — account sign-in.
  • Telegram — message delivery.
  • Google — Google Sheets, where your records are stored.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

We process data to perform our contract with you (providing the service), on our legitimate interests (security, preventing abuse, improving reliability), and with your consent where required (connecting your Google account). You can withdraw consent at any time.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention

  • Account, agent settings and encrypted tokens: while your account is active.
  • Draft records: removed automatically shortly after they expire.
  • Photos, PDFs and voice recordings: not stored.
  • Rows written to Google Sheets live in your own spreadsheet and are controlled by you.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email support@vadapter.online. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. You can also:

  • disconnect Google in the platform settings;
  • disconnect an agent made by someone else, or delete all your data, on the “Connected agents” page, or send /stop to the bot;
  • revoke Vadapter AI’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions;
  • ask us to delete your account and all associated data.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Google tokens and conversation drafts are encrypted at rest. Access to production systems is limited to people who need it.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The platform uses only cookies and storage that are strictly necessary for sign-in.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. For significant changes we will notify account owners by email.
