Set it up once. Then just message it.

You describe your sheet one time. After that, adding a row is a chat message.

  1. Connect Google and pick a sheet

    Choose the one spreadsheet the agent may use. It doesn’t look at the rest of your Drive.

  2. Describe your columns

    Amount, date, category, client: whatever you track. Dropdown lists from your sheet are picked up automatically.

  3. Message the bot

    Write, talk or send a photo in Telegram. Check the card, reply “yes”, and the row is saved.

No new app to learn and no template to switch to. The agent works with your columns, your lists and your formulas.

  • Picks the right tabExpenses go to Expenses, sales go to Sales. One message can fill both.
  • Writes only where you saidRows go into the columns you configured. Formula columns stay untouched.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You pick a sheet, name your columns and share a bot link. That’s the whole setup.

Can it see my other Google files?

No. You choose one spreadsheet and the agent works with that file only. You can revoke access at any time in your Google account.

What if it gets something wrong?

Nothing is written until you confirm. Reply with a correction, like “category is travel”, and the card updates. Reply “cancel” to drop it.

Can I use several tabs or sheets?

Yes. One agent can work with up to 10 tabs of a spreadsheet and chooses the right tab for each record. You can create separate agents for separate spreadsheets.

Do I need Telegram?

Today the agent works in Telegram, and account owners can also test it in a web chat on the platform. More messengers are on the way.

How much does it cost?

Pro is $20 per month per workspace, or $200 per year. See pricing.

Where is my data stored?

Your records live in your own Google Sheet. We keep your account settings and encrypted access tokens on our servers. See the Privacy Policy for everything we store and why.
